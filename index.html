Stonkulator

Stonkulator is a Python game that runs fully in the terminal, where you trade stocks for a living. Buy low, sell high, repeat. Reach milestones to get higher share limits. Progress is saved automatically every 5 minutes. You can also create custom difficulties and stocks to make the game harder or funner.
